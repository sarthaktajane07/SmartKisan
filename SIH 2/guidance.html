1
Soil Preparation

Essential Steps

  • Test soil pH and nutrient levels
  • Remove weeds and debris from field
  • Plow the field to 15-20 cm depth
  • Level the field for uniform water distribution
  • Add organic matter or compost
  • Create proper drainage system

💡 Pro Tips

Soil Testing
Get your soil tested 2-3 weeks before planting to determine exact nutrient requirements.
Organic Matter
Add 5-10 tons of well-decomposed farmyard manure per acre for better soil structure.
2
Seed Selection & Treatment

Seed Requirements

  • Choose certified, disease-free seeds
  • Select varieties suitable for your region
  • Check seed germination rate (minimum 85%)
  • Treat seeds with fungicides
  • Use proper seed rate per acre
  • Store seeds in cool, dry place

💡 Pro Tips

Seed Treatment
Treat seeds with Trichoderma or Pseudomonas for natural disease resistance.
Variety Selection
Choose high-yielding, disease-resistant varieties recommended for your area.
3
Sowing Guidelines

Sowing Instructions

  • Sow at recommended depth (2-3 cm)
  • Maintain proper row spacing
  • Use seed drill for uniform sowing
  • Sow during optimal weather conditions
  • Cover seeds with fine soil
  • Apply light irrigation after sowing

💡 Pro Tips

Timing
Sow during early morning or evening to avoid heat stress on seeds.
Spacing
Proper spacing ensures adequate sunlight and air circulation for healthy growth.
4
Irrigation Management

Water Requirements

  • Provide light irrigation immediately after sowing
  • Maintain soil moisture at 60-70%
  • Irrigate every 7-10 days during growth
  • Increase frequency during flowering
  • Reduce irrigation before harvest
  • Use drip irrigation for water efficiency

💡 Pro Tips

Water Conservation
Use mulching to retain soil moisture and reduce irrigation frequency.
Timing
Irrigate early morning to minimize water loss through evaporation.
5
Fertilizer Application

Nutrient Management

  • Apply basal dose of NPK fertilizers
  • Use organic fertilizers for better soil health
  • Apply nitrogen in split doses
  • Use micronutrients as needed
  • Apply fertilizers at root zone
  • Follow soil test recommendations

💡 Pro Tips

Organic Options
Use vermicompost and bio-fertilizers for sustainable farming practices.
Timing
Apply fertilizers during active growth periods for maximum absorption.
6
Pest & Disease Management

Protection Measures

  • Monitor crops regularly for pests
  • Use integrated pest management (IPM)
  • Apply biological control methods
  • Use chemical pesticides as last resort
  • Maintain field hygiene
  • Follow proper spray timing

💡 Pro Tips

Natural Methods
Use neem oil and other botanical pesticides for eco-friendly pest control.
Early Detection
Regular field visits help in early detection and control of pest problems.
